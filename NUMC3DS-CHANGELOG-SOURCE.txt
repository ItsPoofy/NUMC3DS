NUMC3DS "1.9.20" Snapshot 2

THIS MOD IS IN HEAVY DEVELOPMENT AND ITS MODIFICATIONS ARE EXTREMELY DEEP AND INTRICATE. DO NOT USE ON REGULAR WORLDS WITHOUT BACKING UP FIRST (USE THE "EXPORT WORLD" BUTTON). PLEASE REPORT ANY BUGS OR CRASHES.

KNOWN ISSUES:
SOME COMMANDS MAY BE UNSTABLE ON REAL HARDWARE.
SOME UIs ARE STILL WIP AND MAY BE UNREFINED.

Changelog:

Added:

- Chat, alongside a new Chat button.
- A custom keyboard for Chat and commands, including sent-message history, chat-history pages, cursor controls, symbol input, and command autocomplete.
- Quick Commands, allowing quick access to World Spawn, Teleport, Time, and Weather commands.
- 39 commands based on MCPE 1.1.5. Four server-only commands are intentionally excluded: listd, setmaxplayers, transferserver, and wsserver.
- Command blocks, alongside a recreated command-block screen.
- Cheats controls in the Create World, Edit World, and in-game Options menus.
- Achievement eligibility banners and warnings in the Create World and Edit World menus.
- World importing and exporting, including progress and replacement handling.
- More graphical settings, including FOV, Fancy Graphics, Beautiful Skies, Hide Hand, Hide HUD, and Profiler.
- Maps and Locator Maps, along with their crafting recipes and original cartographer trades. Held maps replace the minimap display and maps can be displayed in item frames.
- New world loading and leaving screens.
- Integrated Cracko's optimized shaders and clouds for performance.
- Restored the missing "Leader of the Pack" achievement.
- Restored missing Long Mundane Potion and Splash Long Mundane Potion variants in Creative inventory.
- Restored missing Stronghold chest loot tables matching MCPE 1.1.5
- Restored offhand slot with full functionality for totems, arrows and mending.
- The ability to import and export worlds straight to SDMC by putting them in minecraft 3DS/importedworlds and exporting to minecraft 3DS/exportedworlds
- The ability to import custom skins via minecraft 3DS/skins, with it appearing in a "custom" skinpack. The skin works on multiplayer without other player having to install that specific skin.
- The ability to detect and connect to LAN MCPE/Windows 10 1.1.5 clients, and to have them join you back, as well as joining servers. Note that 3DS players now have to have NUMC3DS in order to play together.
- Added brand new splash text.
Changed:

- Brightness and FOV settings now display their current values.
- Always Day is now tied to Cheats.
- Removed the eight-world creation and loading restriction.
- Various improvements to performance.
- Various adjustments to UI.
- Removed the ability to trigger totems by simply having them in your inventory.
- Removed the mob cap

Fixed:

- Corrected the original mob animation speed.
- Stopped empty boats from retaining player paddle input.
- Stopped fishing hooks from repeatedly striking an already attached entity.
- Fixed random minimap corruption.
- Fixed sign text editing restriction from 3 lines to 4 lines
